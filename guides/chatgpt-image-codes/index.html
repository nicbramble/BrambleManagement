Your next post does not need a bigger idea. It needs a stronger visual direction. This free six-page pack gives you 20 memorable style codes and the full prompts that make each one work.

What is inside

Use /cinematic, /editorial, /product, /stopmotion, /polaroid, /bluehour, /goldenhour, /macro, /isometric, /flatlay, /retroprint, /neon, /minimal, /surreal, /luxury, /comic, /paperart, /tinyworld, /explodedview, and /beforeafter.

How to use the codes

These are creator-made prompt shortcuts, not built-in ChatGPT commands. Pick a code, replace the bracketed details in its expanded prompt, and paste the full prompt into ChatGPT. The guide also includes a simple formula for building your own styles.

Get a better image

Start with a clear subject, purpose, and style. Add the framing, lighting, and constraints that matter. Once the core idea looks right, make one targeted revision at a time and say what should stay exactly the same.

Make it yours

Use the examples as a starting point, then swap in your business, products, customers, colors, and story. A repeatable visual language makes your content recognizable before anyone reads the caption.
