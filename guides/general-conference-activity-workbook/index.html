Free General Conference activity workbook

A free 25-page printable activity workbook for kids ages 8-11, with listening games, drawing prompts, talk notes, puzzles, and simple ways to remember what they learn.

Download the free workbook (PDF) ↓
View resource artwork

This free printable workbook gives kids ages 8-11 something meaningful to do before, during, and after general conference. They can move through it at their own pace, take breaks, and choose the pages that help them listen.

What is inside

  • Conference bingo and a listening scavenger hunt
  • Pages for drawing messages and turning stories into comics
  • Simple talk notes, scripture spotting, and word activities
  • Puzzles, reflection pages, and small action plans
  • A grown-up guide and answer key

Print and use it

Print the workbook on US Letter paper at Actual Size (100%). Use single-sided pages for easy writing, or print double-sided with long-edge binding. Grab pencils or crayons, choose an activity, and make the book your own.

This is an independent activity workbook and is not an official publication of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.
