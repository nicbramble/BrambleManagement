The four-part prompt that gets useful work

A reusable framework for giving AI enough context to produce something you can actually use.

Start the guide ↓
View resource artwork

A good prompt does not need to be long. It needs to remove ambiguity.

Use four parts

  1. Role: Who should the AI act like?
  2. Context: What does it need to know about your business and audience?
  3. Task: What exact outcome do you want?
  4. Constraints: What should the answer include, avoid, or sound like?

The template

Act as [role]. Here is the relevant context: [context]. Create [specific deliverable]. The result must [constraints and quality bar].

Save this structure and fill it in instead of starting every prompt from scratch.
